## 🚀 Setup Google Search Console

### Step 1: Verify Your Site
1. Go to **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about)**
2. Click **"Start now"** or **"+ Add property"**
3. Choose **Domain** property type
4. Enter your domain: `digitalcowboy.com`
5. You'll see a DNS record to add to your domain registrar

   **DNS Verification (Recommended):**
   - Login to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
   - Add the TXT record provided by Google
   - Wait for DNS propagation (up to 48 hours)
   - Click **Verify** in Google Search Console

   **Alternative: HTML File Upload**
   - Download the HTML verification file from Google
   - Upload to your `/public` directory as `[verification-file].html`
   - Google will verify automatically

6. Once verified, you'll see your property dashboard

### Step 2: Submit Your Sitemap
1. In Google Search Console, go to **Sitemaps** (left sidebar)
2. In the "New sitemap" field, enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**
4. Google will crawl and index your pages

### Step 3: Submit Your Robots.txt
1. Robots.txt is automatically discoverable at: `https://digitalcowboy.com/robots.txt`
2. You can verify it in Google Search Console → **Settings** → **Review requested changes**

### Step 4: Monitor Your Site
- **Coverage Report**: See which pages are indexed
- **Performance**: View click-through rates, impressions, and rankings
- **Mobile Usability**: Check mobile compatibility
- **Core Web Vitals**: Monitor page speed and user experience
- **Links**: See internal and external links
- **Enhancements**: Check breadcrumbs, FAQ, etc.
